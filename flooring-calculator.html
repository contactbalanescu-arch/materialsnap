How this is calculated

Room Area = Length × Width. Flooring is estimated at 20 sq ft per box, with 10% extra added for cuts and waste, then rounded up. Underlayment is estimated separately at 100 sq ft per roll.

FAQ

How much does one box of flooring cover?
About 20 sq ft per box on average — check your box label for the exact coverage.

Do I need underlayment?
Recommended under most laminate and vinyl flooring, unless it's pre-attached.

Should I buy extra flooring?
Yes — 10% extra is already included for cuts and waste.
