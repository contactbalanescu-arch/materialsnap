How this is calculated

Volume = Length × Width × Depth. We convert depth from inches to feet, calculate cubic feet, then convert to cubic yards (÷27) and round up to the nearest quarter yard. Bags are estimated at one standard 2 cubic ft bag per calculation.

FAQ

How deep should mulch be?
Typically 2-3 inches for most garden beds, up to 4 inches for weed suppression.

How many bags do I need?
Standard mulch bags hold 2 cubic feet each.

Bulk or bags?
Bulk is usually cheaper for large areas; bags are more convenient for small beds.
