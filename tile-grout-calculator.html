How this is calculated

Room Area = Length × Width. Assuming standard 12×12 inch tiles (1 sq ft each), we add 10% extra for cuts and breakage, then round up. Grout is estimated at one 25 lb bag per 70 sq ft for standard joint spacing.

FAQ

What tile size does this assume?
Standard 12×12 inch tiles (1 sq ft each). Adjust proportionally for other sizes.

How much grout do I need?
Roughly one 25 lb bag per 70 sq ft for standard joint spacing.

Should I buy extra tile?
Yes — 10% extra is already included for cuts and breakage.
