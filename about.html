About MaterialSnap

MaterialSnap is a free collection of calculators built for people tackling their own home improvement projects — no contractor, no guesswork, just fast answers.

Whether you're tiling a bathroom, painting a bedroom, pouring a concrete slab, or laying down mulch for spring, MaterialSnap tells you exactly how much material to buy before you head to the store.

Why we built this

Most material calculators online are cluttered with ads or ask you to sign up just to do basic math. MaterialSnap is the opposite: type in your numbers, get your answer, done. No accounts, no popups, no tracking.

How the calculations work

Each calculator uses standard industry coverage rates (like 9 sq ft per roll of sod, or 350 sq ft per gallon of paint) with a small buffer built in for waste and cuts. These are estimates meant to get you close — always check your specific product's label for exact coverage, since materials vary by brand.
